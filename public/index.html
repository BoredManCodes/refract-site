Rotating admin

A new Head Admin every 3 months.

Terms run 90 days. Players nominate and vote, and the winner takes over the moment voting closes. Nobody holds power for good, and the outgoing Head Admin steps back to Admin or to player.

There is no store.

Refract will never be pay to win. The server sells nothing for real money.

The only shops are in game, run by players who sell items to other players. Everything else comes from playing and from voting.

Vote for the server, get rewarded.

Voting earns rewards in game. Vote links and the reward list are posted in Discord before launch.

Time in the world counts.

Time spent playing earns rewards in game. The playtime tiers are posted in Discord before launch.

The Head Admin has real power and a short leash.

The Head Admin answers to the Owner. Sensible ideas go ahead. Anything that affects players is posted to the public admin log with a reason.

Without asking

  • Add or remove Admins and Staff, with a reason posted publicly
  • Change visible, reversible gameplay settings: spawn, events, build rules
  • Ban or mute players for breaking posted rules
  • Run events, wipe small areas, update the server rules

Needs the Owner first

  • A world reset, map wipe or Minecraft version change
  • Anything that costs money
  • Free items, ranks or permissions outside an announced event
  • A permanent ban of a non-obvious rule-breaker, or any ban of staff
  • Changing these rules or the election rules

Never

  • Take or spawn items, ranks or permissions for themselves or friends
  • Use admin powers to win a dispute they are part of
  • Delete or edit the admin log
  • Ignore a vote result or hold power past the term
Sensible versus crazy

An idea is sensible if it applies to everyone, can be undone, costs no money and no one's progress, and its reason fits in two sentences and is posted publicly. Fail any check and it is crazy. Wiping one player's base, giving a friend free diamonds and deleting the log are crazy.

Disputes go to the Owner, who decides within 48 hours and posts the answer. Repeated or deliberate breaches get the Head Admin removed mid-term, with a public reason.

Pick three. Points decide.

Ballots are anonymous and cannot be changed. You cannot vote for yourself. At least two candidates must run, or the election is cancelled and the Owner decides.

To run or vote
✓ In the server for 30 days or more
✓ Minecraft account linked through DiscordSRV
Ties
  1. Most points wins
  2. Then most 1st-place votes
  3. Then most 2nd-place votes
  4. Then a 48 hour runoff between the tied candidates
Worked example · 5 ballots
Candidate A11 pts · 3 first
Candidate B8 pts · 1 first
Candidate C6 pts · 1 first
Candidate D5 pts · 0 first

1st choice earns 3 points, 2nd earns 2, 3rd earns 1. The winner takes Head Admin the moment voting closes. The outgoing Head Admin becomes Admin, or a player if they step down.

Run by a group of old mates.

We met years ago on a Minecraft server that did not last. We stayed in contact, kept playing together, and decided to build the server we wanted. That server is Refract SMP.

Because we have watched a server fall apart, the rules here are written to stop that: one owner, a Head Admin who rotates, and no store to fall out over.
