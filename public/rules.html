Player rules

These rules apply on the Minecraft server and in the Discord. Joining either one means you accept them. Staff enforce them, and bans and other actions that affect players are posted to the public admin log with a reason.

1. Respect other players

  • No harassment, threats, doxxing, or targeted abuse of any player or staff member.
  • No hate speech or slurs, and no discrimination based on race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or nationality.
  • No sexual content. Sexual content involving minors is an immediate permanent ban and a report to the platform.
  • Disagreeing is fine. Following someone around to keep arguing is harassment.

2. Chat

  • No spam, flooding, excessive caps, or repeating the same message.
  • No advertising other servers, Discords, channels or stores, and no sending invite links in private messages.
  • Use English in public chat so staff can moderate it. Other languages are fine in private messages.
  • Do not impersonate staff or other players.
  • Do not share or ask for another player's personal information.
  • Do not bypass the chat filter with spacing, symbols or misspellings.

3. Fair play

The server sells nothing for real money, so every advantage comes from playing. An advantage you get without playing is cheating.

  • No hacked clients or cheat mods. This covers x-ray, kill aura, fly, speed, auto-clickers, scaffold, freecam and similar tools, plus x-ray texture packs.
  • Client mods that only change what you see are allowed: shaders, performance mods, zoom, and minimaps without entity radar or cave finding. If you are unsure about a mod, ask in Discord before you use it.
  • No exploiting bugs. Report them in Discord. Using a bug for gain counts as cheating, and anything gained from it is removed.
  • No duplicating items. Using a known dupe, or sharing how to do it, is a ban.
  • No lag machines, and no builds or farms made to slow or crash the server.
  • No macros or AFK tools to dodge AFK limits or to earn playtime rewards without playing.

4. Griefing and stealing

  • Do not destroy, alter, burn, flood or build over another player's builds without their permission.
  • Do not take items from another player's chests, farms or storage without their permission, even if the container is unprotected.
  • Do not trap or repeatedly kill players who have not agreed to fight. Player versus player is allowed only where both players have opted in.
  • Do not use lava, TNT, fire or mob farms to damage land you do not own.
  • Staff restore griefed builds and return stolen items from backups. The griefer loses what they took and is banned.

5. Building

  • No offensive structures, including hate symbols and sexual builds.
  • Do not build next to another player's base in a way that blocks its view, access or spawn.
  • Clean up abandoned builds, floating trees and lava casts when you leave an area.
  • Keep entity counts down. Staff remove excess animals, mobs and item frames that hurt performance, with notice where possible.

6. Trading

  • Real-money trading is banned. Do not buy or sell in-game items, ranks, accounts or currency for money, gift cards, crypto or anything else of value outside the game.
  • Scamming in player trades is a ban. A trade stands once both players accept, so read what you accept.
  • Do not offer or accept items in exchange for breaking rules, such as paying someone to grief.
  • Staff close player shops that sell stolen or duplicated items.

7. Accounts and elections

  • One person, one active account. Alternate accounts are allowed for a second device or a rename, but you must tell staff, and an alternate account cannot vote, farm rewards, or evade a punishment.
  • Link your Minecraft account to Discord with /discord link. New players and unlinked players get stricter moderation.
  • Do not share your account. You are responsible for everything done on it.
  • Vote rewards and playtime rewards belong to the account that earned them. Bots, scripts and extra accounts used to inflate either are banned.
  • Do not manipulate Head Admin elections. Vote buying, multiple votes, and pressuring or bribing voters void the votes and lead to a ban.

8. Staff

  • Follow staff instructions. If you think a decision is wrong, comply first and appeal after.
  • Do not argue punishments in public chat. Use the appeal process below.
  • Do not harass staff or try to bait them into breaking rules.
  • Staff cannot give you items, ranks or permissions outside an announced event, and cannot punish you in a dispute they are part of.

9. Punishments

Punishment depends on the offence, the player's history, and whether it was deliberate. Staff can skip steps for serious offences.

OffenceTypical first action
Chat spam, minor language, minor advertisingWarning, then mute
Harassment, abusive language, repeated advertisingMute or temporary ban
Griefing, stealing, exploit useTemporary ban and rollback
Hacked clients, duplication, real-money tradingPermanent ban
Threats, doxxing, sexual content involving minors, ban evasionPermanent ban

Evading a ban with another account extends the original ban and bans the new account. A permanent ban of a non-obvious rule-breaker, and any ban of a staff member, needs the Owner's approval.

10. Appeals

To appeal, open a ticket in the Discord or message an Admin. Give your Minecraft username, the punishment, and what happened from your side. An Admin who was not involved in the original decision reviews it. If you still disagree, the Owner decides within 48 hours and posts the answer. Appeals made through harassment, threats or ban evasion are rejected.

11. Platform rules and changes

You must follow the Minecraft EULA and Usage Guidelines, and the Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. You must own a legitimate copy of Minecraft Java Edition.

The Owner approves changes to these rules. Changes take effect when posted, and the date at the top of this page shows the latest version. Staff can act on plainly harmful behaviour that is not listed here, and post the reason to the public admin log.
